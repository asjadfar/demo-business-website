Baked fresh, served with a smile

Golden Crust started in 2015 with one oven, a family recipe book, and a simple promise: nothing leaves our kitchen unless we would serve it at our own table.

Today we bake over 40 items daily, from crusty sourdough to birthday cakes, and brew coffee from locally roasted beans.
